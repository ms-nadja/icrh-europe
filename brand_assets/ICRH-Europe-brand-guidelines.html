Overview

Two voices, one conversation

The mark itself is the brief: two figures in dialogue, framed by overlapping speech bubbles, held inside a single navy circle. Every choice below extends that idea — a confident, institutional blue grounded by a warm, human gold, with the cooler bubble tones doing the quiet work of structure and hierarchy across the site.

Usage

Color pairings

Backgrounds and the text colors that stay readable on top of them.

On navy

Use white or powder blue for text. Gold only for small accents, never body copy — contrast is too low for long text.

On gold

Navy deep is the only text color that passes contrast comfortably on gold. Avoid white or light blue.

On paper

Default body copy is ink on paper. Navy for headings, slate blue for supporting UI like tags and icons.

Typography

Logo

Logo usage

The mark always sits inside its white ring on any background darker or busier than plain paper. Keep the two figures and both speech bubbles fully visible — they're the whole idea.

Minimum clearspace: half the mark's diameter on every side. Minimum display size: 32px.

Do

Use on white, paper, or navy backgrounds with clear space around it.

Don’t

Recolor the mark, stretch it, drop the white ring, or place it on busy imagery.

Voice

Voice & tone

Direct, not clinical

Plain sentences over institutional jargon. Say what happens, not how the system works.

Warm, not casual

Respectful of the subject matter. Friendly without slang or forced enthusiasm.

Inclusive by default

Written for a cross-border, multilingual audience — short sentences translate better.
