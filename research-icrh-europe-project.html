Project description

From bouncing back to bouncing forward

As an understanding of organisational resilience evolves beyond "bouncing back" from a setback to continuous adaptation and "bouncing forward", the processes and systems that underpin ongoing organisational resilience are increasingly coming more to the fore.

This project sets out to explore dynamic internal narratives and interactions that may support organisational resilience. In particular, it takes a closer look at internal communication practitioner experiences and practices related to communicating with employees in times of frequent change.

Leading questions

What the research is asking

How is resilience understood by senior leaders?

How does internal communication help organisations to become more resilient?

What role do internal narratives play in times of multiple change programmes?

What role do personal interactions between senior leaders and employees play in times of multiple change programmes?

Methodology

Where the fieldwork is happening

To answer these research questions, the team is conducting interview and focus group studies with internal communication practitioners across Europe – 48 interviews and one focus group in each of the following countries.

Austria Czech Republic Denmark France Germany Italy Netherlands Poland Spain Switzerland UK Ukraine
