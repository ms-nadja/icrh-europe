Research

ICRH Europe supports research that moves between evidence and practice. Two studies are currently underway – a global benchmark of excellence in internal communication, and grounded fieldwork on internal communication and organisational resilience across Europe.

Global study

ICRH Global Excellence Study

A mixed-method study involving more than 20 researchers across five regions, building a contemporary, globally grounded framework for excellence in internal communication.

Read about the study
Europe-wide fieldwork

ICRH-Europe research project

Internal Communication and Organisational Resilience: interviews and focus groups with practitioners in 12 European countries, exploring how organisations "bounce forward" in a permacrisis world.
